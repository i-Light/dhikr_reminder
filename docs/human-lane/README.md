## Issue to post (H4)

Post this on `https://github.com/rn0x/Adhkar-json/issues` as a new issue.

> **Title:** Which licence applies to the adhkar text and audio?
>
> Hello, and thank you for collecting this. I make a small free dhikr reminder
> app (no ads, no accounts). The README says the files can be used freely, but
> there is no LICENSE file, so I cannot tell what is allowed.
>
> Could you add a licence file, or tell me in this issue: (1) may the Arabic text
> be used inside an app with attribution? (2) may the audio files be used? (3) if
> yes, what wording of credit do you want?
>
> I will not ship anything from this repo without a clear answer. I only use it
> to cross-check typos in my own list. Thank you.

Then, in a browser, read `https://sunnah.com/about` (the part on reproduction,
copying and scraping) and paste its exact words into
`docs/research/content-sources.md`, under the Sunnah.com row. Our fetch tool got a 403.

## Five questions (H3)

Send to 10 to 20 people who use, or would use, a dhikr app. One message, answers by
voice note are fine.

**Arabic (Egyptian):**

> السلام عليكم. بشتغل على تطبيق ذكر بيفكّرك بالأذكار. ممكن تجاوبني على 5 أسئلة
> قصيرة؟
> 1. إيه أكتر حاجة بتضايقك في تطبيقات الأذكار؟
> 2. لو التطبيق بيعمل حاجة واحدة بس تفيدك، تبقى إيه؟
> 3. حصل إن التذكيرات وقفت عندك من نفسها؟ إمتى؟
> 4. ناقصك إيه في التطبيق؟
> 5. ودجت (أيقونة على الشاشة الرئيسية) تعدّ لك الذكر بضغطة، هتستفيد منها؟
>
> وبصراحة لو في حاجة في التطبيق معقدة عليك قولّي.

**English:**

> Hi. I make a small dhikr reminder app. Could you answer five short questions?
> 1. What annoys you most in dhikr apps?
> 2. If the app did only one thing well for you, what would it be?
> 3. Did reminders ever stop on their own? When?
> 4. What is missing?
> 5. Would a home-screen widget that counts one dhikr per tap help you?
>
> And please tell me honestly if anything in the app feels complicated.

If you can, also watch one parent or friend open the app for the first time, say
nothing, and write down where they hesitate. That is worth more than the answers.

## Message for testers (H13)

First check in Play Console, Testing, whether your account still needs 12 testers
for 14 days (new personal accounts did in 2024 and 2025). If it does:

> السلام عليكم. محتاج 12 حد يجرّب تطبيق ذكر على أندرويد لمدة 14 يوم عشان
> أقدر أنشره على Play. هتدخل على اللينك ده بإيميل Gmail بتاعك، تنزّل التطبيق
> وتسيبه شغّال. مفيش حاجة تانية مطلوبة منك. جزاك الله خير.
>
> (the closed-test link from Play Console goes here)

## Store registration (H7, optional, only if you want the Microsoft Store)

Individual registration is free. Start at `https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard`,
pick "App developer", "Individual". It asks for an ID and a selfie, and verification
takes days. You do not need to do anything else until the roadmap reaches W12.
