Re-check by: 2027-04-09 (apps change fast; redo with Google Play reviews filtered for undo, widget and vibration)

## Features that competing apps offer

- Counting: a large tap target, volume-button counting (contested: one developer refused it as unsuited to continuous use; others such as myTasbih offer it), adjustable haptic strength, optional count sounds.
- Safety against mistakes: one-tap undo, reset away from the main tap area, undo of a reset instead of a confirmation popup, a screen lock against accidental taps.
- Targets: personal goals, a pinned favourite goal on the main screen, a configurable starting count, phased presets such as 33/33/33 with swipe or auto-advance to the next.
- Tracking: daily and weekly stats, lifetime totals, best streaks, consecutive-day streaks.
- Surfaces: home-screen widgets for favourite dhikr, pinned counters opened from widgets, a Wear OS counter with wrist motion and a progress ring.
- Comfort: counts saved automatically and the app reopens where the person stopped, offline use, no account, dark mode, multiple counters or folders, a privacy mode that hides the number, RTL support.

## What users complain about

- Ads, by a wide margin. One reviewer reported an ad every 14 seconds, which made worship hard. Minimal, ad-free apps are praised.
- Alerts that fail silently: a reviewer said there was no sound or vibration at the target number and the toggles seemed to do nothing. The developer's reply was that the phone's silent mode blocks alerts. Lesson: tell the person when silent mode is why a cue did not play.
- Accidental taps on menus while counting.
- Audio that stops when the phone auto-locks (one reviewer).
- Hard-to-navigate favourites (one reviewer).
- A reviewer wanted a real widget showing the count to remind forgetful users of their goals, and shortcuts to saved tasbihs for different occasions.

## Gaps we can fill (and where they are planned)

- Reminder-first design with a real card on the screen (no competitor found does interval cards on Windows and Android): already the core of the app.
- Reliable Android delivery with a visible health check: release 0.2.
- No ads, no accounts, private streaks, no sharing of progress: ground rules.
- Counter with undo, haptics, lock, rounds and sequences: release 1.1.
- Widget, Quick Settings tile, notification actions, launcher shortcuts: release 1.2.
- Windows global hotkey counting (not seen elsewhere): release 1.1.

## Not copied, on purpose

- Badges, leaderboards, sharing progress: against the no-show-off rule.
- Counting with the screen off through a media-session trick: Play reviews it badly.
- Wear OS: parked until a volunteer maintainer exists.
