# Content and Dataset License (Adhkar, Translations, and Virtues)

## 1. Sacred Texts (Quranic Verses and Prophetic Hadiths)

The Arabic texts of Quranic verses and authentic Prophetic supplications (*adhkar* and *duas*) are part of the shared Islamic heritage and are in the **public domain** worldwide. No person or entity claims copyright over the words of the Quran or the Sunnah.

## 2. Compiled Dataset Structure, Transliterations, Translations, and Reflections

To ensure this work serves as an enduring *Sadaqah Jariyah* (ongoing charity) that any Muslim developer, researcher, or community can freely mirror, port, print, or rebuild for decades to come, all original compilation work in this repository (including JSON/Dart schemas, transliterations, English and Egyptian Arabic UI strings, benefit summaries, and reflections) is dedicated under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)**, with permission to also use under **CC0-1.0 Public Domain Dedication** where attribution is technically impractical (such as minimal embedded devices or watch faces).

### Scholarly Integrity Guidelines for Forks and Mirrors

While not a legal restriction on open-source freedom, anyone reusing or adapting the adhkar dataset is earnestly asked to uphold three scholarly safeguards:

1. **Preserve Authentic Wording and Vocalisation (*Tashkeel*)**: Do not alter Quranic verses or Prophetic wording without verifying against primary sources (*Sahih al-Bukhari*, *Sahih Muslim*, the *Sunan*, and *Hisn al-Muslim*).
2. **Keep Sources and Grades Attached**: Retain hadith references alongside virtues (*fadl*) and never ship unreviewed AI-generated religious rulings or fabricated virtues.
3. **Keep Remembrance Free of Exploitation**: Honour the spirit of `WAQF.md` by never placing advertisements, paywalls, or user tracking around the remembrance of Allah.
